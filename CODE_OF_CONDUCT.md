# Code of conduct

We want this to be a useful place for people learning and working in tech.

## Expected

- Be respectful. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume people are here to learn. Explain instead of dismissing.
- Give credit for ideas and contributions.
- Keep discussions on the topic of the repo.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language of any kind.
- Posting private information about someone without their consent.

## Report

Open a private report through GitHub's report feature on the content, or contact the repository owner. Reports are reviewed by the maintainer. Repeated or serious violations can lead to a block from the repo.
